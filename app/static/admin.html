Reference accuracy % is from an external benchmark (per-task, not measured against this app's own pipeline). Speed and cost figures were measured here in an internal benchmark, using the app's real Excel-level Build prompt: "first answer" is the time to the first response, and cost is the total for one conversation of 3 rounds (about 3 requests). The app tries this model first and, if it fails, the fallback models set in .env (OPENROUTER_FALLBACK_MODEL, currently DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017, then Claude Haiku 5.5). Two Ling models and GLM 5.3 were tried and left out: the Ling models often timed out or returned unusable answers, and GLM 5.3 cost about ten times more per conversation.
